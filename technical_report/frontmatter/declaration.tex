\chapter*{Report Basis and Scope}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Report Basis and Scope}

This report is a faithful consolidation of the source dataset, preprocessing code,
versioned experiment protocols, saved machine-readable results, and verification
artifacts present in the project workspace on 6 September 2026. The original
proposal and the course slides supplied by Dr. Sibasankar Padhy are treated as
contextual academic sources, not as instructions to claim work that was not run.

The five-member table on the title page and the proposal-assigned responsibilities
in Chapter~\ref{ch:scope} are transcribed from the supplied formal proposal. They
record the planned module allocation; they are not used to infer undocumented
individual contribution percentages.

All reported predictive results are subject-level cross-validation estimates from
one public cohort. They are not external validation, medical-device evidence, or
clinical diagnostic performance. The strongest observed result, 59.7\% mean
balanced accuracy for the tuned RBF SVM family, is explicitly secondary to the
predeclared V4 automatic-selection endpoint of 54.5\%.

\begin{cautionbox}{Integrity note}
No source BIDS recording, earlier processed partition, model artifact, or prior
result was deleted or silently overwritten during the project. Corrections to
electrode order and boundary handling are documented as corrections, and earlier
outputs are retained as historical exploratory evidence.
\end{cautionbox}
